You can delete your account and personal data at any time, straight from the app or with the form below.

01Before you delete

Deletion is permanent. Remaining points are forfeited and cannot be restored, even if you sign up again with the same email. If you have a pending withdrawal, wait until it shows Done before you delete your account.

02Delete in the app (fastest)

This works the same in the Android app and in the web app at halago.online. Your account is deleted immediately.

  1. Open HalaGo and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. You will be signed out and your account will be deleted.

03Request by form

No longer have the app? Fill in the form below with the email address of your HalaGo account. We process requests within 7 days and may email you to confirm that the account is yours.

Account deletion requestOpen in a new tab

If the form does not load, open it in a new tab, or email support@halago.co from your account email with the subject “Delete my HalaGo account”.

04What gets deleted

Deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, photo and avatar
  • Your points balance and levels
  • Your sign‑in and notification settings

Kept

  • Withdrawal and transaction records, for accounting
  • One‑way hashes of devices and browsers linked to fraud, so banned users cannot sign up again

Data that partners collected when you used their offers is held by them. Contact them directly to delete it. More details are in our Privacy Policy.
